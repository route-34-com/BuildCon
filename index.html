Questions we hear every week.

Still unsure? Call +91 98430 34034 or message us on WhatsApp.

How much does it cost to build a house in Coimbatore or Erode?

Our turnkey rates start at ₹1,950/sq.ft (Essential), ₹2,350/sq.ft (Signature) and ₹2,900/sq.ft (Luxe) in Coimbatore; Erode is about 4% lower. A 2,000 sq.ft Signature home is roughly ₹47 lakh including structure, finishes, electrical and plumbing. Use the estimator above for a quick figure.

How long will my house take?

A typical G+1 home of 2,000–2,500 sq.ft takes 8–10 months from groundbreaking. Larger homes take 10–14 months. The handover date goes into your contract with a rent-back commitment if we're late.

Do you handle DTCP / LPA and building permit approvals?

Yes. Our liaison team prepares drawings and files for building permits with Coimbatore and Erode corporations, panchayats and DTCP/LPA. Average turnaround is 38 days, and approval fees are passed on at actuals.

How does the payment schedule work?

After a 5% booking amount, the remaining amount is split across 14 milestones — footing, plinth, each slab, brickwork, plastering, flooring and so on. You pay only after a stage is completed and passes our QA inspection.

Can you help with a home loan?

Yes. We provide the approved drawings, BOQ, cost estimate and stage-wise disbursement schedule that banks ask for, and our team coordinates site visits with your bank's engineer.

Can I choose my own tiles, fittings or brands?

Absolutely. Each package comes with allowances (for example, ₹100/sq.ft for flooring in Signature). Choose anything at our Peelamedu experience centre — if it costs more, you pay only the difference.

I live abroad. Can I still build with you?

Many of our clients are NRIs. You can approve designs over video call, watch the site on 24×7 CCTV, sign documents digitally and pay milestones online. A relative can collect keys on your behalf at handover.

Which areas do you serve?

Coimbatore city and suburbs including Saravanampatti, Vadavalli, Kovaipudur, Singanallur, Pollachi and Mettupalayam; and Erode district including Thindal, Perundurai, Bhavani, Gobichettipalayam and Chennimalai. We also take projects in Tiruppur.
